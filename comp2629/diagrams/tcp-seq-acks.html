Two ways to feel how TCP labels every byte and confirms what arrived. Drive the sliding window yourself, then watch the telnet echo exchange play out.

The sliding window

A bigger byte stream, ten 50-byte segments. The seq number of each segment is the offset of its first byte. Send, ACK, and try dropping a segment to see out-of-order delivery and cumulative ACKs.

One keystroke, three segments

You type a single character in a telnet session. Watch how seq and ack numbers reference each other as the byte travels to Host B and the echo comes back.
